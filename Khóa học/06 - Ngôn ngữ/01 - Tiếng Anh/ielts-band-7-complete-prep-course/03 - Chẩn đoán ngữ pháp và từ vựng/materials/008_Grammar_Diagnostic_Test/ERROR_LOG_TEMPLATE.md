## Nhóm lỗi gợi ý

- Modifiers: adjective/adverb, articles, comparatives/superlatives, `too/very/enough`, determiners.
- Verbs: tense, conditionals, modals, participles, gerunds/infinitives, time clauses.
- Pronouns: case, number, person, relative pronouns, reference.
- Basic patterns: word order, indirect objects, embedded questions, clauses, double subjects.
- Style/usage: preposition combinations, parallelism, wordiness, voice, agreement, commonly confused words.
- Lexical resource: word choice, collocation, word family, register, repetition.
