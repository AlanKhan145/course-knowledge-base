Core patterns

Ôn nhanh trước khi làm bài

Các điểm ngữ pháp và collocation quan trọng nhất của Module 07.

Home vs house

house → công trình vật lý
home → nơi mình sống/cảm giác thuộc về

Nhớ: go home, không dùng go to home.

There is / There are

There is + danh từ số ít
There are + danh từ số nhiều

There is a sofa. · There are two armchairs.

Adjective order

Size/Quality + Color + Material + Noun

a large brown wooden table · a small black leather sofa

Position

next to · between · behind · in front of · opposite · against the wall

Describe a room

room → overall feeling → furniture → position → favourite detail

Learn chunks

a high ceiling · a small balcony · a marble countertop · a fully furnished apartment

Prompt A · My home

  • Do you live in a house or an apartment?
  • What rooms does it have?
  • What do you like most about it?

Mục tiêu: 1 loại nhà + 3 phòng/khu vực + 3 tính từ + 2 collocation.

Prompt B · Favourite room

  • What is your favourite room?
  • What furniture is in it?
  • Where are the main objects?

Mục tiêu: There is/are + ≥4 đồ vật + ≥2 giới từ vị trí + ≥2 tính từ.

01:00

Prompt C · Ideal home

  • What would your ideal home be like?
  • How many rooms would it have?
  • What materials/colors would you choose?

Mục tiêu: detached house/apartment + spacious/bright/airy + materials/colors + natural light.
