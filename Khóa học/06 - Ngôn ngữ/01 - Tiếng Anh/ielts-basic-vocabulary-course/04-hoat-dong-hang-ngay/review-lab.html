Key distinctions

Các cụm dễ nhầm

Đây là những điểm cần phân biệt trước khi làm bài: hành động bắt đầu, hành động hoàn tất và kết quả thực tế.

wake up ≠ get up

wake up = tỉnh giấc; get up = ra khỏi giường.

I wake up at 6:30, but I get up at 6:45.

make breakfast ≠ have breakfast

make = chuẩn bị; have/eat = ăn.

I make breakfast, then I have breakfast.

go to bed ≠ fall asleep

go to bed = lên giường; fall asleep = bắt đầu ngủ.

I go to bed at 10 but fall asleep at 10:30.

homework ≠ housework

homework = bài tập; housework = việc nhà.

I do my homework, then I do some housework.

go home

Không dùng go to home. home ở đây là trạng từ chỉ hướng.

I finish work at five and go home.

go for a walk ≠ go for a run

walk = đi bộ; run = chạy để tập thể dục.

I often go for a walk after dinner.

Grammar focus

Present Simple cho thói quen

Dùng Present Simple cho hành động lặp lại, lịch sinh hoạt và sự thật thường xuyên.

Loại câuI / you / we / theyhe / she / it
Khẳng địnhI wake up at six.
They go to bed at ten.		She wakes up at six.
He goes to work by bus.
Phủ địnhI don't drink coffee.She doesn't drink coffee.
Câu hỏiDo you have breakfast?Does she start work at eight?
Nhớ: sau does hoặc doesn't, động từ trở về nguyên mẫu: Does she start? / She doesn't work.

Reading practice

Đọc hiểu về daily routines

Đọc đoạn văn trước, sau đó trả lời câu hỏi. Hai bài giữ lại các cụm từ và cấu trúc chính của module.

Reading 1 · Morning Routine

Rocio's morning

Hello, my name is Rocio, and I am from El Salvador. In the morning, I wake up at 6:00 a.m. I brush my teeth and wash my face. Then I eat breakfast. I like eggs with toast and avocado. I don't drink coffee, so I drink water or orange juice. After breakfast, I get ready to start work at 7:00 a.m. I work from home, so I don't take the bus or drive my car. During the morning, I also take a break. Sometimes I go for a walk with my dog around the park.

Reading 2 · Work-from-home Routine

Nerrisa's workday

Nerrisa starts her day at seven o'clock in the morning. She works from home, and her work starts at eight. She has one hour to prepare. She washes her face, makes coffee, and checks her schedule on the computer. During her free time, she enjoys hobbies such as coloring and playing online games. She also cooks and cleans her home. In the afternoon, she takes a nap and a shower before working again at 5:00 p.m. She finishes work at 9:00 p.m. Her whole day usually finishes at around 11:00 in the evening.
