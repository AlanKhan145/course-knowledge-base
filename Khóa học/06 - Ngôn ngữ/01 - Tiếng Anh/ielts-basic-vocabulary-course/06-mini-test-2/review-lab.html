True / False

Đọc tình huống ngắn và xác định phát biểu đúng hay sai.

A busy Saturday

On Saturday, Linh wakes up at 7:30. She has breakfast with her parents and then washes the dishes. At 10:00, she goes shopping with her sister. In the afternoon, the family prepares dinner together because their aunt and uncle are coming to visit. Linh makes a cake while her father follows a recipe for soup. They have dinner at 7:00 p.m. There are some leftovers, so Linh puts them in the fridge. She goes to bed at 11:00.

Part K · Speaking

Speaking 45–60 giây

Luyện nói sau khi hoàn thành phần từ vựng để chuyển từ nhận biết sang sử dụng chủ động.

Speaking 1 · Daily Routine & Housework

  • What time do you wake up and get up?
  • What do you usually do before breakfast?
  • What housework do you do on weekdays?
  • What is different about your weekend routine?
  • When do you usually go to bed?

Mục tiêu: ≥6 từ/cụm mục tiêu, ≥3 collocation, ≥2 trạng từ/từ chỉ tần suất.

wake up · get up · have breakfast · take a shower · make the bed · wash the dishes · sweep · take out the rubbish · go to bed

Speaking 2 · Family & Meals

  • Who do you live with?
  • Which relative are you closest to and why?
  • How often does your family eat together?
  • Who usually prepares dinner?
  • What happens to leftovers after a meal?

Mục tiêu: ≥6 từ về family/meals, ≥3 collocation, ≥1 câu so sánh hoặc mô tả quan hệ.

parent · relative · spouse · meal · leftovers · prepare dinner · follow a recipe · close relatives
