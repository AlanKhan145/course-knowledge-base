Part 3 · Conversation set C01 (3 câu)

Woman: Did you send the revised schedule to the clients?
Man: Not yet. I am waiting for the room numbers from the reception desk.
Woman: I just received them. I will forward the list now.

  1. What are the speakers discussing?
  2. What information is the man waiting for?
  3. What will the woman probably do next?
Answer key: revised schedule · room numbers · forward the list. Khi thêm audio, giữ mã C01.
Part 4 · Talk set T01 (3 câu)

Good morning, passengers. The 8:30 train to Riverside will leave from platform 4 instead of platform 2. Please check your tickets before boarding. The café near platform 4 will remain open until 9:00.

  1. Where would listeners hear this announcement?
  2. What has changed?
  3. What will remain open?
Answer key: a station · the platform · the café. Khi thêm audio, giữ mã T01.

Short answer

Short-answer QuestionsTrue / False / Not GivenYes / No / Not Given

TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN

TRUE: bài đồng ý với thông tin.
FALSE: bài nói điều ngược lại.
NOT GIVEN: bài không đủ thông tin.

YES / NO / NOT GIVEN

Dùng khi kiểm tra quan điểm/claim của tác giả, không chỉ một sự kiện thực tế. Đừng đổi hai loại này cho nhau.

Mini passage · practice

Urban gardens have become common in cities where residents have limited access to private outdoor space. Some projects grow vegetables, while others focus on flowers and community events. Researchers often examine whether the gardens improve social contact as well as local biodiversity. However, a garden does not automatically benefit every resident: opening hours, location and maintenance can affect who uses it.

1. The passage says all urban gardens grow vegetables.

2. The author believes access conditions can influence garden use.
