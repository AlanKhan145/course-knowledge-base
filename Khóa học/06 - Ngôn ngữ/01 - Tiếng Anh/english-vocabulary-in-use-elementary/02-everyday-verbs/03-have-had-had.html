Core idea

Have → had → had

Học cả cụm thay vì dịch từng từ: have breakfast = ăn sáng, không phải “có bữa sáng”.

Retrieval cue: Khi thấy một danh từ như breakfast, hãy tự hỏi: “Động từ tự nhiên đi cùng nó là gì?”

02 · Learn

Hiểu hệ thống trước khi luyện

Giữ form, nghĩa và ngữ cảnh trong cùng một mental model.

Forms of have

haveI / you / we / they
I have breakfast at 7.
hashe / she / it
She has lunch at school.
hadpast: mọi chủ ngữ
We had dinner yesterday.
Verb line: have → had → had. Past participle cũng là had: I have had breakfast.

Ba cách dùng không nên trộn lẫn

PatternÝ nghĩaExampleGợi ý nhớ
have + nouncó / sở hữuI have a car.một vật, một người
have + activity nounthực hiện hoạt độngI have breakfast at 7.học cả collocation
have to + verbphải / cần phải làmI have to study.sau to là verb nguyên mẫu

Affirmative, negative, questions

Positive

She has a car.

They had dinner.

Sau chủ ngữ ngôi 3 hiện tại: has.

Negative

She doesn't have a car.

They didn't have breakfast.

Không dùng: doesn't has / didn't had.

Questions

Does she have a car?

Did you have breakfast?

Rule: sau do / does / did luôn dùng have.

Have got và have to

Have got = possession

I have a pen. ≈ I've got a pen.

She's got blue eyes.

Scope: have got thường dùng cho sở hữu, không thay thế mọi collocation.

Tự nhiên: I have breakfast at 7.
Không nên: I have got breakfast at 7.

Have to = obligation

I have to study tonight.

She has to get up early.

We had to wait for an hour.

Pattern: have / has / had to + verb nguyên mẫu.
Collocations

Học theo nhóm ngữ nghĩa

Images giúp gợi cảnh; chunk giúp gợi cách dùng.

Examples in daily life

Morning

I get up at 6:30. I have a shower and then have breakfast. I usually have a drink before I go to school.

Evening

I have dinner with my family. After dinner, I usually study. When I am tired, I have a rest.

Weekend

We sometimes have a party at home. We listen to music, eat together and have a good time.

Điểm dễ nhầm

✗
Does she has breakfast?

Sau does, dùng have.

✓
Does she have breakfast?

Auxiliary đã mang dấu hiệu ngôi 3.

✗
Did you had dinner?

Sau did, dùng have.
