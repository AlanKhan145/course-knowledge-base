# Bộ prompt mẫu

## Template tổng quát

```text
[NAME OF FOOD],
[visible ingredients + texture],
[plating + vessel],
[camera angle + framing],
[lighting],
[background / environment],
[purpose: menu / advertising / editorial / social],
[realism cues]
```

## Realism cues

```text
natural imperfections
subtle asymmetry
realistic food texture
restrained highlights
natural shadows
irregular crumbs
subtle moisture
documentary realism
shallow depth of field
```

## Appetite cues

```text
subtle steam
melted cheese
glossy sauce
crisp edge
soft crumb
freshly toasted surface
condensation
gentle dripping
```

## Góc máy

- `45-degree three-quarter view`
- `close three-quarter angle`
- `eye-level food photography`
- `slightly elevated 35-degree angle`

## Ánh sáng

- `soft window daylight`
- `diffused side light`
- `warm restaurant side lighting`
- `soft studio daylight`
- `moody directional light`

## Background

- `clean neutral tabletop`
- `subtle cafe background`
- `dark casual restaurant setting`
- `rustic kitchen table`
- `simple pastel backdrop`
