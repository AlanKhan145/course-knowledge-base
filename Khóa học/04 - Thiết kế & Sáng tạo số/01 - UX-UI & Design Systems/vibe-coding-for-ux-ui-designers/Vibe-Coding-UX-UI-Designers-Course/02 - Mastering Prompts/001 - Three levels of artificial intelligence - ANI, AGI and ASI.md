## Main Idea

Introduces the classic AI capability ladder -- Artificial Narrow Intelligence (today's tools, including all the ones used in this course), Artificial General Intelligence, and Artificial Super Intelligence -- to frame what today's AI design tools can and can't do. By the end of the lesson, learners should see how Three levels of artificial intelligence - ANI, AGI and ASI connects to designing and shipping AI-assisted UX/UI work without traditional coding.

* ANI vs. AGI vs. ASI
* Current AI capability boundaries
* Why this matters for prompting expectations
