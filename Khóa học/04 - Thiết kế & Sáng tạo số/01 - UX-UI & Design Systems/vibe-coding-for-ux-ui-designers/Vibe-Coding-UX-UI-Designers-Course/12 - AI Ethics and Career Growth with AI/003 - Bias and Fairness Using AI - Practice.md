## Section

AI Ethics and Career Growth with AI

## Main Idea

Explains how bias in training data can surface in AI-generated personas, imagery, or copy, and practical checks designers can run to catch unfair or unrepresentative output. By the end of the lesson, learners should see how Bias and Fairness Using AI connects to designing and shipping AI-assisted UX/UI work without traditional coding.

## Key Topics Mentioned

* Sources of AI bias
* Fairness checks
* Representative output
