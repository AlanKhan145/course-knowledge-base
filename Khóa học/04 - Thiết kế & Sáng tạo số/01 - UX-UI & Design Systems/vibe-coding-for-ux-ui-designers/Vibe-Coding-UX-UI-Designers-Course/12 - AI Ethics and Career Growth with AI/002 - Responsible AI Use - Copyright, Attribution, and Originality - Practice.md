# 002 - Responsible AI Use - Copyright, Attribution, and Originality

## Section

AI Ethics and Career Growth with AI

## Duration

Not listed

## Main Idea

Covers the legal and ethical questions around AI-generated design assets and copy -- who owns the output, when attribution is needed, and how to avoid passing off AI work as fully original without disclosure. By the end of the lesson, learners should see how Responsible AI Use - Copyright, Attribution, and Originality connects to designing and shipping AI-assisted UX/UI work without traditional coding.

## Key Topics Mentioned

* Copyright of AI output
* Attribution practices
* Originality concerns
