# 003 - Figma Make, Figma Slides and Figma Buzz

## Section

Figma Project: Prototyping and animation

## Duration

2:15

## Main Idea

Introduces three Figma AI products: Figma Make (prompt-to-app/site generation), Figma Slides (AI-assisted presentation building), and Figma Buzz (on-brand content generation), and where each fits in a designer's toolkit. By the end of the lesson, learners should see how Figma Make, Figma Slides and Figma Buzz connects to designing and shipping AI-assisted UX/UI work without traditional coding.

## Key Topics Mentioned

* Figma Make
* Figma Slides
* Figma Buzz
* Choosing the right Figma AI product

Introduces three Figma AI products: Figma Make (prompt-to-app/site generation), Figma Slides (AI-assisted presentation building), and Figma Buzz (on-brand content generation), and where each fits in a designer's toolkit.
