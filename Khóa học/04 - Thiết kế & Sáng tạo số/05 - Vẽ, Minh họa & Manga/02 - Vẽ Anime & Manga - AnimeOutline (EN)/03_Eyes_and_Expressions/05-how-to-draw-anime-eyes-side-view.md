## Learning objective

Simplify the eye to a readable profile form.

## Core principle

A strong drawing is easier to edit when large masses, directional guides, and relationships are established before any fine rendering. For **anime eyes in side view**, treat each visible line as evidence of an underlying form or a deliberate graphic design choice. Begin lightly, compare against a reference, and separate the *rough construction* from the *refined final drawing*.

## Practice assignment

**Main exercise:** Draw one calm and one surprised profile eye.

- **Warm-up (5 min):** Draw several quick studies using only large shapes and gesture lines.
- **Construction (10–15 min):** Produce a rough version with visible guides and a consistent reference.
- **Refinement (10–15 min):** Correct the largest issues, remove unnecessary guides, and strengthen important contours.
- **Review (5 min):** Compare both stages, write down two improvements and one point to practice again.

## Common pitfall

**Watch out for:** Placing the iris as a complete front-view circle in profile.

Correct the issue in the rough phase rather than hiding it with extra contour lines or shading. A useful test is to shrink your drawing to thumbnail size; major shapes should remain intelligible.
