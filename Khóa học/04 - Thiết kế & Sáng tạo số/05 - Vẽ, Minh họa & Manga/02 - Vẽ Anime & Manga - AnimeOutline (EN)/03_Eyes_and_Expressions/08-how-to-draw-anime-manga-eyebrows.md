# 23. Anime Eyebrows

> **Module:** 03 Eyes & Expressions  
> **Level:** Beginner  
> **Suggested practice:** 25–45 minutes  
> **Original source:** [Anime Eyebrows](https://www.animeoutline.com/how-to-draw-anime-manga-eyebrows/) · AnimeOutline

## Learning objective

Use brow tilt, height, and curvature to control expression.

## Core principle

A strong drawing is easier to edit when large masses, directional guides, and relationships are established before any fine rendering. For **anime eyebrows**, treat each visible line as evidence of an underlying form or a deliberate graphic design choice. Begin lightly, compare against a reference, and separate the *rough construction* from the *refined final drawing*.

## Practice assignment

**Main exercise:** Create eight eyebrow studies with the same eyes.

- **Warm-up (5 min):** Draw several quick studies using only large shapes and gesture lines.
- **Construction (10–15 min):** Produce a rough version with visible guides and a consistent reference.
- **Refinement (10–15 min):** Correct the largest issues, remove unnecessary guides, and strengthen important contours.
- **Review (5 min):** Compare both stages, write down two improvements and one point to practice again.

## Common pitfall

**Watch out for:** Assuming eyebrows must be mirror images for every emotion.

Correct the issue in the rough phase rather than hiding it with extra contour lines or shading. A useful test is to shrink your drawing to thumbnail size; major shapes should remain intelligible.
