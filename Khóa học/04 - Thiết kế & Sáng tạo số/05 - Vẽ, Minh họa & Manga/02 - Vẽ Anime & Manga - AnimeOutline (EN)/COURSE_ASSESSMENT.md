# Portfolio-based course assessment

## Milestone A — Head and expression (after Modules 1–3)

Create a character head sheet with front, side, and three-quarter views, plus six expressions. The eyes and facial landmarks should stay consistent across the sheet.

## Milestone B — Full body and costume (after Modules 4–6)

Produce a front and side reference sheet, four hand gestures, and one dynamic pose. Add a garment that follows the body, with a small number of believable folds.

## Milestone C — Finished illustration (after Module 7)

Complete a full-body character inside a basic perspective environment. Use a limited palette and a clear main light direction. Keep the rough construction as part of your portfolio.

## Final project — A short manga sequence (after Module 8)

Plan and draw a two- to four-page original one-shot with a clear beginning, turn, and ending. Provide character design notes, rough page thumbnails, clean panels, speech bubbles, and final artwork.

## Assessment rubric (0–4 points each)

| Criterion | 0 | 2 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structure | Unclear main masses | Mostly consistent | Consistent and intentionally designed |
| Proportion | Severe mismatches | Minor discrepancies | Controlled stylization |
| Expression / pose | Difficult to read | Understandable | Immediately readable |
| Composition | Weak focus | Some hierarchy | Strong eye path and clear focus |
| Craft | Rough, incomplete | Partially polished | Clean and expressive |

Score out of **20** and record one concrete revision target. Keep rough and refined stages as separate evidence.
