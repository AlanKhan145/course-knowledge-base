# 40. Anime Arms

> **Module:** 05 Body Hands & Poses  
> **Level:** Beginner–Intermediate  
> **Suggested practice:** 25–45 minutes  
> **Original source:** [Anime Arms](https://www.animeoutline.com/how-to-draw-anime-arms/) · AnimeOutline

## Learning objective

Construct arms with joint positions and tapering volumes.

## Core principle

A strong drawing is easier to edit when large masses, directional guides, and relationships are established before any fine rendering. For **anime arms**, treat each visible line as evidence of an underlying form or a deliberate graphic design choice. Begin lightly, compare against a reference, and separate the *rough construction* from the *refined final drawing*.

The instructions below are an **independently written practice companion**, not a transcription or complete substitute for the original tutorial. Follow the site for its specific demonstrations and step sequences.

## Guided workshop

1. **Place shoulder, elbow, and wrist points first.** After the step, compare the silhouette and relative placement to your reference.
2. **Draw upper and lower arm segments as tapered cylinders.** After the step, compare the silhouette and relative placement to your reference.
3. **Rotate the forearm to study changing silhouette.** After the step, compare the silhouette and relative placement to your reference.
4. **Connect the hand without a sharp wrist break.** After the step, compare the silhouette and relative placement to your reference.

## Practice assignment

**Main exercise:** Draw four arm gestures from simple cylinders.

- **Warm-up (5 min):** Draw several quick studies using only large shapes and gesture lines.
- **Construction (10–15 min):** Produce a rough version with visible guides and a consistent reference.
- **Refinement (10–15 min):** Correct the largest issues, remove unnecessary guides, and strengthen important contours.
- **Review (5 min):** Compare both stages, write down two improvements and one point to practice again.

## Common pitfall

**Watch out for:** Positioning the elbow halfway along the entire hand-to-shoulder reach without checking pose.

Correct the issue in the rough phase rather than hiding it with extra contour lines or shading. A useful test is to shrink your drawing to thumbnail size; major shapes should remain intelligible.
