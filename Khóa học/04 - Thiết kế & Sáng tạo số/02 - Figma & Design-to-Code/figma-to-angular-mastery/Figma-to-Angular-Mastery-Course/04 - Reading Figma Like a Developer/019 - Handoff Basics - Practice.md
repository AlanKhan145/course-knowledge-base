# 019 - Handoff Basics – Designer vs developer translation

## Section

Reading Figma Like a Developer

## Duration

1:55

## Main Idea

This lesson reframes the design handoff moment as a translation problem between two ways of thinking: a designer describes a screen in terms of visuals and intent, while a developer needs to think in terms of components and code. It covers the kinds of clarifying questions worth asking a designer before starting a build -- about states, edge cases, and responsive behavior -- and encourages looking at any layout with an eye toward its underlying structure rather than just its surface appearance. Building this translation habit is what makes the rest of the section's mapping work possible. It's the mindset shift that turns "looking at a design" into "planning a build."

## Key Topics Mentioned

* Design handoff as a translation problem
* Questions worth asking a designer before building
* Looking at a layout in terms of components, not just visuals
* Anticipating states and edge cases early
* Shifting from viewing a design to planning a build
