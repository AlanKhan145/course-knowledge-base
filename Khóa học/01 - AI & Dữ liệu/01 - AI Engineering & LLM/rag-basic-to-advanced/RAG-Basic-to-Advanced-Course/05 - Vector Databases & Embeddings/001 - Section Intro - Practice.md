# 001 - Section Intro

## Section

Vector Databases & Embeddings

## Duration

1 minute

## Main Idea

Introduces the core technical section of the course. Explains that understanding vectors and embeddings is the key to understanding why and how RAG retrieval works.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Know what topics the section covers: vectors, embeddings, multiple data modalities, vector DB indexing, and Pinecone.
- Understand that this section is the theoretical and practical core of RAG.

## Summary

This section builds the foundation for all retrieval in RAG. Without understanding how text (and other data) becomes vectors, and how those vectors are stored and searched, the pipeline is a black box. This section opens that box.
