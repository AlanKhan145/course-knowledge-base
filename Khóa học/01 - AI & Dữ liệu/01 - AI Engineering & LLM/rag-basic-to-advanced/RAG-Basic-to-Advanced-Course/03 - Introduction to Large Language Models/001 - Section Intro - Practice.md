# 001 - Section Intro

## Section

Introduction to Large Language Models

## Duration

1 minute

## Main Idea

Brief orientation to the LLM section: why understanding LLMs is essential for building RAG systems, and what topics the following lessons will cover.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Understand why LLMs are the "generation" half of RAG.
- Know what to expect from the upcoming lessons on closed-source models, open-source models, and running LLMs locally.

## Summary

This intro frames LLMs as the component that takes retrieved context and produces a human-readable answer. The section will compare closed-source (API-based) and open-source (locally run) LLMs and explain how RAG improves the quality of LLM outputs.
