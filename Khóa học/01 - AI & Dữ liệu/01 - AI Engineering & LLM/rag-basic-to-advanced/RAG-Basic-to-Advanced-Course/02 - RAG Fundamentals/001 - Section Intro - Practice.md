# 001 - Section Intro

## Section

RAG Fundamentals

## Duration

1 minute

## Main Idea

Brief overview of what the RAG Fundamentals section will cover: core concepts, comparison with traditional methods, real-world applications, and the end-to-end architecture of a RAG system.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Know what to expect from the RAG Fundamentals section.
- Understand why this conceptual foundation matters before writing any code.

## Summary

This short intro orients students to the upcoming five lessons covering what RAG is, how it compares to older approaches, where it is used in industry, and how the full pipeline fits together.
