# 001 - Introduction

## Section

Introduction

## Duration

2 minutes

## Main Idea

This lesson welcomes students to the course and sets expectations for what they will build and learn. The instructor explains that the course takes a practical, project-driven approach to Retrieval-Augmented Generation, ending with three production-ready chatbots: a Website Chatbot, a SQL Chatbot, and a Multimedia PDF Chatbot.

## Course Overview

This course focuses on **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** — the technique of combining large language models with external data retrieval so that answers are grounded in specific, up-to-date information rather than solely in the model's training weights.

Students will progress from RAG fundamentals through vector databases, LangChain pipelines, and advanced techniques, culminating in real project builds.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Understand the overall purpose and scope of the course.
- Identify the three capstone projects (Website Chatbot, SQL Chatbot, PDF Chatbot).
- Recognize that the course spans both theory and hands-on implementation.
- Set expectations for the journey from RAG basics to production deployment.

## Key Topics Mentioned

- Retrieval-Augmented Generation overview
- LangChain as the orchestration framework
- Vector databases and embeddings
- Open-source LLMs via Ollama
- Commercial LLMs (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini)
- Production chatbot projects

## Why This Lesson Matters

This lesson frames the entire course. Understanding what you are building toward — and why RAG solves real problems — makes every subsequent technical lesson more meaningful.

## Review Questions

1. What is the main problem that RAG solves for LLMs?

## Summary

The course teaches RAG from first principles to production deployment. Students will use LangChain, vector databases, and both local and commercial LLMs to build chatbots that answer questions grounded in real, retrievable data.
