Welcome to the Blender Mega Course!

If you ever wanted to create 3D models and environments but felt overwhelmed by Blender,

this class is the perfect place to start.

My name is Caroline Conte, I'm a 3D artist with 7 years of experience working with Blender

for stylized environments, architectural visualization, and also 3D game art.

Over the years, I worked on everything from simple beginner projects in Blender to more

advanced ones, and in this class, I'm going to guide you through a Blender beginner-friendly

workflow, so you can learn Blender by creating 3D from start to finish.

Blender is an amazing 3D software with tons of features.

It already helped me by map creating, logo animation, video editing, and of course, innumerous

3D scenes and environments.

In this course, you will build a complete fantasy cabin scene, while learning the core

fundamentals of Blender.

We will start by getting to know the interface, the navigation, and how to transform your objects.

Then, we will get to know the fundamentals of 3D modeling, like vertex, edges, and faces.

Also, its modifiers and simple hard surface techniques.

As we progress, we will start to build the fantasy cabin itself, its structure, adding

details, and organizing our scene properly.

After that, we will bring our scene to life, adding materials, light, setting up the camera,

and our render techniques, to have an amazing polished image at the end.

This project won't require any external assets, so everything that will build all of the textures

will be the default built-in procedural textures inside Blender.

By the end of this class, you will understand the core Blender workflow, and feel confident

to create your own 3D models.

Let's get started!
