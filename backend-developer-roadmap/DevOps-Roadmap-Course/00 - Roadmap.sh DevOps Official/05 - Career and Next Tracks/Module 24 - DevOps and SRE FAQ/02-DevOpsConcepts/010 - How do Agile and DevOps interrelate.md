## 1. Tom tat

Bai nay giai thich **How do Agile and DevOps interrelate?** trong boi canh DevOps/SRE hien dai. Sau bai hoc, ban nen biet no la gi, nam o dau trong workflow van hanh va cach bien no thanh thuc hanh faq nho.

## 2. Muc tieu hoc tap

- Tra loi duoc cau hoi: How do Agile and DevOps interrelate?
- Dua ra duoc vi du thuc te thay vi chi noi dinh nghia.
- Lien ket cau tra loi voi delivery, reliability, security hoac collaboration.

## 3. Khai niem chinh

- Cau hoi nay kiem tra cach ban hieu DevOps trong boi canh thuc te: delivery, reliability, automation va collaboration.
- Cau tra loi tot nen ngan, co vi du, co trade-off va tranh noi DevOps chi la danh sach tool.
- Hay chuan bi mot cau chuyen that ve pipeline, incident, automation hoac cai thien van hanh.
