## 1. Tom tat

Bai nay giai thich **What Is DevOps** trong boi canh DevOps. Ghi chu tu roadmap: DevOps is a cultural and collaborative mindset emphasizing communication, collaboration, integration, and automation between development and operations teams for faster and more reliable software delivery.

## 2. Muc tieu hoc tap

- Giai thich duoc What Is DevOps bang ngon ngu cua ban.
- Nhan biet no xuat hien o dau trong DevOps workflow thuc te.
- Ap dung vao mot demo, command, config, pipeline hoac runbook nho.

## 3. Khai niem chinh

- DevOps la su ket hop giua van hoa cong tac, tu dong hoa, feedback nhanh va trach nhiem van hanh ro rang.
- Muc tieu khong phai chi la dung tool moi, ma la giao phan mem on dinh hon voi cycle time ngan hon.
- DevOps tot can do duoc flow, quality, reliability, security va kha nang rollback.
