## 1. Tom tat

Bai nay giai thich **DevOps Engineer** trong boi canh DevOps. Ghi chu tu roadmap: A software engineer specializing in best practices and tools that enable continuous delivery; bridges development and operations, automates releases, manages infrastructure, CI/CD, monitoring, and reliability.

## 2. Muc tieu hoc tap

- Giai thich duoc DevOps Engineer bang ngon ngu cua ban.
- Nhan biet no xuat hien o dau trong DevOps workflow thuc te.
- Ap dung vao mot demo, command, config, pipeline hoac runbook nho.

## 3. Khai niem chinh

- DevOps engineer noi giua development, infrastructure, security va operations.
- Cong viec thuong gom CI/CD, cloud, IaC, monitoring, logging, incident response va platform enablement.
- Gia tri lon nhat la lam delivery lap lai duoc, quan sat duoc va it phu thuoc thao tac tay.

## 4. Vi du / Demo

```text
Input: mot thay doi nho trong he thong
Process: ap dung khai niem cua bai hoc vao script, config, pipeline hoac runbook
Output: ghi lai ket qua, loi gap phai va cach debug
```

## 5. Bai tap thuc hanh

- Viet 5 dong tom tat bai hoc khong nhin tai lieu.
- Tao vi du nho bang command, config, script, dashboard hoac runbook.
- Tim mot loi production co the lien quan va viet cach debug.

## 6. Loi thuong gap

- Hoc thuoc dinh nghia nhung khong tao vi du.
- Bo qua edge case vi demo nho van chay.
- Khong ghi lai cau hoi con mo de quay lai sau.

## 7. Checklist hoan thanh

- Toi co the giai thich **DevOps Engineer** trong 1-2 phut.
- Toi co mot vi du nho hoac ghi chu thuc hanh cho bai nay.
- Toi biet bai nay lien quan den delivery, operations, reliability, security hoac automation nao.
- Toi da ghi lai it nhat mot cau hoi can tim hieu sau neu con mo ho.

## 8. Outcome lien quan

Explain what DevOps means, how it differs from a tool checklist, and what an operations-focused engineer owns.

## 9. Tong ket

**DevOps Engineer** la mot moc trong lo trinh DevOps. Hay bien no thanh mot script, mot config, mot dashboard, mot pipeline, hoac mot runbook nho de kien thuc co cho bam.
