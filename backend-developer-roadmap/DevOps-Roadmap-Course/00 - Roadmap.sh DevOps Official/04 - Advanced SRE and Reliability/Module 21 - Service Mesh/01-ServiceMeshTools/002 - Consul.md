## 1. Tom tat

Bai nay giai thich **Consul** trong boi canh DevOps/SRE hien dai. Sau bai hoc, ban nen biet no la gi, nam o dau trong workflow van hanh va cach bien no thanh thuc hanh service mesh nho.

## 2. Muc tieu hoc tap

- Giai thich duoc Consul bang ngon ngu cua ban.
- Nhan biet no xuat hien o dau trong DevOps workflow thuc te.
- Ap dung vao mot demo, command, config, pipeline hoac runbook nho.

## 3. Khai niem chinh

- Consul xu ly mot so van de service-to-service nhu mTLS, routing, retry va telemetry.
- Mesh co ich khi so service, policy va traffic pattern du phuc tap.
- Can can nhac overhead, debug complexity va ownership truoc khi dua vao production.
