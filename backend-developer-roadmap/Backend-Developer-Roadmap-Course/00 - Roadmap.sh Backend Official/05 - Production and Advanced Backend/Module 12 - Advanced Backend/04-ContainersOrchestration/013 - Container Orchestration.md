## 1. Tom tat

Bai nay giai thich **Container Orchestration** trong boi canh backend hien dai. Sau bai hoc, ban nen nam khai niem, biet vi sao no quan trong va co mot bai tap nho de ap dung.

## 2. Muc tieu hoc tap

- Giai thich duoc Container Orchestration bang ngon ngu cua ban.
- Nhan biet khi nao kien thuc nay xuat hien trong backend project.
- Thuc hanh mot vi du nho va ghi lai loi thuong gap.

## 3. Khai niem chinh

- Orchestration quan ly nhieu container tren nhieu node.
- No xu ly scheduling, service discovery, scaling, rollout va self-healing.
- Can hoc khi app vuot qua single-server/container don le.

## 4. Vi du / Demo

```text
Input: mot yeu cau backend nho
Process: ap dung khai niem trong bai vao API, database, queue hoac deployment
Output: ghi lai ket qua, loi gap phai va cach debug
```

## 5. Bai tap thuc hanh

- Viet 5 dong tom tat bai hoc khong nhin tai lieu.
- Tao vi du nho trong sandbox backend hoac ghi pseudo-code.
- Tim mot loi production co the lien quan va viet cach debug.

## 6. Loi thuong gap

- Tin output AI ma khong validate schema/permission.
- Khong do latency, token cost va failure rate.
- Log prompt/output co du lieu nhay cam.

## 7. Checklist hoan thanh

- Toi co the giai thich **Container Orchestration** trong 1-2 phut.
- Toi co mot vi du nho hoac ghi chu thuc hanh cho bai nay.
- Toi biet bai nay lien quan den API, database, security, deployment hoac operations nao.
- Toi da ghi lai it nhat mot cau hoi can tim hieu sau neu con mo ho.

## 8. Tong ket

**Container Orchestration** la mot moc trong lo trinh backend. Hay bien no thanh mot vi du nho, mot checklist debug, hoac mot project mini de kien thuc co cho bam.
