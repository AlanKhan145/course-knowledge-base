## 1. Tom tat

Bai nay giai thich **Neo4j** trong nhom **Graph DBs** cua module **Advanced Backend**. Sau bai hoc, ban nen biet vai tro cua no va cach no xuat hien trong he thong backend.

## 2. Muc tieu hoc tap

- Giai thich duoc Neo4j bang ngon ngu cua ban.
- Nhan biet khi nao kien thuc nay xuat hien trong backend project.
- Thuc hanh mot vi du nho va ghi lai loi thuong gap.

## 3. Khai niem chinh

- Neo4j la graph database pho bien voi Cypher query language.
- Phu hop relationship-heavy domain nhu recommendation, fraud, network.
- Can hieu node, relationship, label va traversal.

## 4. Vi du / Demo

```text
Input: mot yeu cau backend nho
Process: ap dung khai niem trong bai vao API, database, queue hoac deployment
Output: ghi lai ket qua, loi gap phai va cach debug
```

## 5. Bai tap thuc hanh

- Viet 5 dong tom tat bai hoc khong nhin tai lieu.
- Tao vi du nho trong sandbox backend hoac ghi pseudo-code.
- Tim mot loi production co the lien quan va viet cach debug.

## 6. Loi thuong gap

- Chon database theo xu huong thay vi access pattern.
- Bo qua index, migration va backup plan.
- Khong do query performance truoc khi toi uu.

## 7. Checklist hoan thanh

- Toi co the giai thich **Neo4j** trong 1-2 phut.
- Toi co mot vi du nho hoac ghi chu thuc hanh cho bai nay.
- Toi biet bai nay lien quan den API, database, security, deployment hoac operations nao.
- Toi da ghi lai it nhat mot cau hoi can tim hieu sau neu con mo ho.

## 8. Tong ket

**Neo4j** la mot moc trong lo trinh backend. Hay bien no thanh mot vi du nho, mot checklist debug, hoac mot project mini de kien thuc co cho bam.
