## 1. Tom tat

Bai nay giai thich **How does the internet work?** trong boi canh backend hien dai. Sau bai hoc, ban nen nam khai niem, biet vi sao no quan trong va co mot bai tap nho de ap dung.

## 2. Muc tieu hoc tap

- Giai thich duoc How does the internet work? bang ngon ngu cua ban.
- Nhan biet khi nao kien thuc nay xuat hien trong backend project.
- Thuc hanh mot vi du nho va ghi lai loi thuong gap.

## 3. Khai niem chinh

- Client, server, DNS, router, ISP va data center tao nen hanh trinh cua mot request.
- Du lieu di qua mang theo packet; backend engineer can hieu latency, bandwidth va reliability.
- TCP/IP dam bao ket noi, HTTP dinh nghia cach client va server trao doi request/response.

## 5. Bai tap thuc hanh

- Tim mot loi production co the lien quan va viet cach debug.

## 6. Loi thuong gap

- Hoc thuoc dinh nghia nhung khong tao vi du.
- Bo qua edge case vi demo nho van chay.
- Khong ghi lai cau hoi con mo de quay lai sau.

## 7. Checklist hoan thanh

- Toi co the giai thich **How does the internet work?** trong 1-2 phut.

- Toi da ghi lai it nhat mot cau hoi can tim hieu sau neu con mo ho.
