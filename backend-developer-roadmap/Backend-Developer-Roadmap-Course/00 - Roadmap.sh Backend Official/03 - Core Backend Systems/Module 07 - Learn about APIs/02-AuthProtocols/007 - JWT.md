## 1. Tom tat

Bai nay giai thich **JWT** trong boi canh backend hien dai. Sau bai hoc, ban nen nam khai niem, biet vi sao no quan trong va co mot bai tap nho de ap dung.

## 2. Muc tieu hoc tap

- Giai thich duoc JWT bang ngon ngu cua ban.
- Nhan biet khi nao kien thuc nay xuat hien trong backend project.
- Thuc hanh mot vi du nho va ghi lai loi thuong gap.

## 3. Khai niem chinh

- JWT la token co header, payload va signature.
- Dung tot cho stateless auth nhung can quan tam expiry, revocation va storage.
- Khong dat secret/private data vao payload vi payload co the decode.

## 4. Vi du / Demo

```http
Authorization: Bearer <access_token>
Cookie: session=<session_id>; HttpOnly; Secure; SameSite=Lax
```

## 5. Bai tap thuc hanh

- Viet 5 dong tom tat bai hoc khong nhin tai lieu.
- Tao vi du nho trong sandbox backend hoac ghi pseudo-code.
- Tim mot loi production co the lien quan va viet cach debug.

## 6. Loi thuong gap

- Tu viet crypto/auth flow khi chua du kinh nghiem.
- Luu secret/token/password sai cho de debug.
- Khong test authorization edge cases.

## 7. Checklist hoan thanh

- Toi co the giai thich **JWT** trong 1-2 phut.
- Toi co mot vi du nho hoac ghi chu thuc hanh cho bai nay.
- Toi biet bai nay lien quan den API, database, security, deployment hoac operations nao.
- Toi da ghi lai it nhat mot cau hoi can tim hieu sau neu con mo ho.

## 8. Tong ket

**JWT** la mot moc trong lo trinh backend. Hay bien no thanh mot vi du nho, mot checklist debug, hoac mot project mini de kien thuc co cho bam.
