# 001 - AI-as-Your-Design-Partner

## Section

AI as Your Design Partner

## Duration

3:42

## Main Idea

Frames AI tools as a collaborative design partner rather than a replacement -- useful for divergent ideation, rapid variation, and unblocking creative decisions, while the designer stays responsible for judgment and taste. By the end of the lesson, learners should see how AI-as-Your-Design-Partner connects to designing and shipping AI-assisted UX/UI work without traditional coding.

## Key Topics Mentioned

* AI as collaborator
* Ideation support
* Designer's role
* Division of labor between human and AI
