# 006 - From Idea to Hypothesis - Structuring Product Thinking Before Generation

## Section

AI as Your Design Partner

## Duration

## Main Idea

Encourages designers to frame a testable hypothesis (who the user is, what problem is being solved, what success looks like) before jumping into AI generation, so prompts and prototypes stay grounded in a real product goal. By the end of the lesson, learners should see how From Idea to Hypothesis - Structuring Product Thinking Before Generation connects to designing and shipping AI-assisted UX/UI work without traditional coding.

## Key Topics Mentioned

* Hypothesis framing
* Problem/user/success definition
* Avoiding generation without direction
